### 5.1 Campaign Structure

**Campaign 1: Search - Brand**
- Keywords: "cơm ánh dương", "com anh duong"
- Budget: 500k/month
- Goal: Protect brand

**Campaign 2: Search - Local Service**
- Keywords: "ship cơm [phường]", "cơm văn phòng [phường]"
- Budget: 3M/month
- Location targeting: 5km radius
- Ad schedule: 9:00-11:00, 15:00-17:00 (decision time)

**Campaign 3: Display - Remarketing**
- Audience: Website visitors, cart abandoners
- Budget: 1M/month
- Creative: Menu highlights + promo

## 7. TRACKING & ANALYTICS

### 7.1 Google Analytics Setup

**Goals:**
1. Order completed
2. Phone call (via call tracking number)
3. Menu viewed
4. Account signup

**UTM Parameters for All Campaigns:**
```
?utm_source=[source]&utm_medium=[medium]&utm_campaign=[campaign]
```

**Example:**
```
comanhduong.com/menu?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=local-search-q1
```

---

### 7.2 Google Search Console

**Monitor:**
- Search queries driving traffic
- Click-through rate (CTR)
- Average position
- Indexed pages

**Monthly Report:**
- Top 20 keywords
- Pages with low CTR → optimize meta descriptions
- Crawl errors → fix

---

### 7.3 GMB Insights

**Track Weekly:**
- Search views (how many saw listing)
- Search queries (what people searched)
- Customer actions:
  - Website clicks
  - Direction requests
  - Phone calls
- Photo views

**Goal:** Increase all metrics by 20% month-over-month
