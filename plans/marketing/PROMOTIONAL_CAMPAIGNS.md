## 1. GRAND OPENING CAMPAIGN (Tuần 1-2)

### Objective:
- First customer acquisition
- Create buzz and awareness
- Database building

### Timeline:
**Soft Launch:** Week 1 (testing, friends & family)
**Hard Launch:** Week 2 (public, paid ads)

---

### Promotion Details:

#### A. First-Timer Discount
**Offer:** GIẢM 20% ĐƠN ĐẦU TIÊN
**Terms:**
- Áp dụng cho khách hàng mới
- Đơn tối thiểu: 50,000đ
- Max discount: 30,000đ
- Code: `CHAOBAN20`

**Mechanics:**
1. Customer đặt đơn đầu tiên
2. Nhập code `CHAOBAN20`
3. Giảm 20% tự động
4. Lưu thông tin vào database (email/phone)

**Budget:** 5,000,000đ (ước tính 167 đơn x 30k discount)

---

#### B. Free Delivery Week
**Offer:** MIỄN PHÍ SHIP TẤT CẢ ĐƠN
**Terms:**
- Áp dụng: Week 1-2
- Không giới hạn số lần
- Bán kính: 5km từ kitchen

**Mechanics:**
- Tự động free ship khi checkout
- Không cần code

**Budget:** 3,000,000đ (1000 đơn x 3k phí ship)

---

#### C. Bundle Deal
**Offer:** COMBO VĂN PHÒNG (5 SUẤT)
**Terms:**
- 5 suất cơm (tự chọn món)
- Giá gốc: 200,000đ
- Giá KM: 150,000đ (Giảm 25%)
- Free ship

**Target:** Company orders, team lunch

**Budget:** 2,000,000đ (40 combo x 50k discount)

---

### Marketing Execution:

**Week 1 (Soft Launch):**
- Organic posts on Facebook

- Free samples to building security
- Flyers tại văn phòng gần

**Week 2 (Hard Launch):**
- Facebook Ads: 3M budget
  - Targeting: 5km radius, 22-45 age

  - Keywords: "cơm ship gần đây", "cơm văn phòng"

**Total Budget:** 10,000,000đ

## 2. LOYALTY PROGRAM (Month 2+)

### Objective:
- Increase repeat purchase rate
- Build customer lifetime value
- Reduce customer acquisition cost

---

### Program Structure: "THÀNH VIÊN NHÀ"

#### Tier 1: BẠC (0-5 đơn)
**Benefits:**
- Tích điểm 5% giá trị đơn hàng
- Birthday gift: 50k voucher
- Email weekly menu trước

**Example:** Đơn 40k → tích 2k điểm

---

#### Tier 2: VÀNG (6-15 đơn)
**Benefits:**
- Tích điểm 8% giá trị đơn hàng
- Free ship tất cả đơn
- Birthday gift: 100k voucher
- Priority delivery
- 1 suất miễn phí/tháng (đơn >50k)

**Example:** Đơn 40k → tích 3.2k điểm + free ship 3k

---

#### Tier 3: KIM CƯƠNG (16+ đơn)
**Benefits:**
- Tích điểm 10% giá trị đơn hàng
- Free ship tất cả đơn
- Birthday gift: 200k voucher
- VIP customer service line
- 2 suất miễn phí/tháng
- Early access menu mới
- Exclusive events (tour bếp, nấu ăn cùng chef)

**Example:** Đơn 40k → tích 4k điểm + free ship + ưu tiên

---

### Points Redemption:
- 100 điểm = 10,000đ giá trị
- Min redeem: 50 điểm (5k)
- Points expiry: 12 tháng

### Special Loyalty Offers:

#### "MUA 10 TẶNG 1"
**Mechanics:**
- Stamp card: Mỗi đơn >40k = 1 stamp
- 10 stamps → 1 suất free (trị giá 40k)
- Digital tracking via app/website

**Budget:** 1,500,000đ/month (assume 300 members x 5k average)

---

#### "THÁNG NÀY ĂN BAO NHIÊU?"
**Mechanics:**
- Top 10 customers by order count/month
- Prize: Free cơm cả tháng sau (1 suất/day)
- Announced end of month

**Budget:** 1,200,000đ/month (10 people x 30 days x 40k = 12M/year)

---

### Implementation:

**Tech Requirements:**
- CRM system to track orders
- Points dashboard (customer portal)
- Automated birthday emails
- Tier upgrade notifications

**Communication:**
- Email: Monthly points statement
- SMS: Tier upgrade congrats
- Push notification: Points expiry warning

**Total Budget:** 5,000,000đ/month
**Expected Impact:**
- Repeat rate: 20% → 50%
- Order frequency: 2x/month → 8x/month
- LTV increase: 3x

## 3. REFERRAL PROGRAM (Month 3+)

### Objective:
- Word-of-mouth acquisition
- Viral growth
- Lower CAC (25k → 15k)

---

### Program: "GIỚI THIỆU BẠN - CÙNG NHẬN QUÀ"

#### Referrer (Người giới thiệu):
**Reward:** 30,000đ voucher
**Terms:**
- Friend must be new customer
- Friend must complete 1st order (min 50k)
- Max 10 referrals/month
- Voucher valid 30 days

---

#### Referee (Người được giới thiệu):
**Reward:** 20,000đ discount on 1st order
**Terms:**
- New customer only
- Min order 50k
- One-time use

---

### Mechanics:

**Step 1:** Customer gets unique referral link
**Step 2:** Share link to friends (Zalo, Messenger, SMS)
**Step 3:** Friend clicks link → tracked by cookie/app
**Step 4:** Friend places order → both get rewards automatically

**Tracking:** UTM parameters + customer ID in URL

**Example URL:**
`https://comanhduong.com?ref=ANH123&utm_source=referral`

---

### Marketing Execution:

**Month 3:**
- Announce program via email to all customers
- Facebook post explaining mechanics
- In-app banner promoting referral
- SMS to top customers

**Ongoing:**
- Reminder emails monthly
- Leaderboard: "Top referrers this month"
- Bonus for top 3: Extra 100k voucher

**Total Budget:** 8,000,000đ
**Expected Referrals:** 400 new customers (200 referrers x 2)
**Expected Revenue:** 32M (400 orders x 80k average - 20k discount)

## 9. RISK MANAGEMENT

### Risk 1: Over-redemption
**Mitigation:** Cap daily redemptions, require min purchase

### Risk 2: Fraud (fake referrals)
**Mitigation:** Verify phone/email, track IP, manual review suspicious

### Risk 3: Budget overrun
**Mitigation:** Daily budget caps, pause campaigns if threshold reached

### Risk 4: Low engagement
**Mitigation:** A/B test offers, increase communication, simplify mechanics
