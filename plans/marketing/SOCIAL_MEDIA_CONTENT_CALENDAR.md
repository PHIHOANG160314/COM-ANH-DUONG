## Content Creation Checklist

### Before Posting:
- [ ] Spell check (Vietnamese + English)
- [ ] Image quality check (min 1080x1080)
- [ ] Link working
- [ ] Hashtags relevant
- [ ] Time scheduled correctly
- [ ] Call-to-action clear

### After Posting:
- [ ] Monitor comments first 30 mins
- [ ] Reply to all comments within 1 hour
- [ ] Track engagement metrics
- [ ] Adjust future content based on performance
