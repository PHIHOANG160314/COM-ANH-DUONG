## 2. STORY IDEAS (Instagram/Facebook Stories)

### Story Series 1: "A Day in the Life"
**Format:** 10-15 stories, time-lapse style

**Content:**
1. 5:00 AM - Alarm clock ⏰
2. 5:30 AM - Chợ sáng 🏪
3. 6:00 AM - Chọn rau tươi 🥬
4. 7:00 AM - Về bếp sơ chế
5. 8:00 AM - Nấu nướng 🍳
6. 9:30 AM - Trang trí món ăn
7. 10:00 AM - First order! 📦
8. 11:00 AM - Packing frenzy
9. 12:00 PM - Shipper xuất phát 🏍️
10. 12:25 PM - Delivered! ✅
11. Customer reaction 😋
12. 8:00 PM - Clean up
13. 9:00 PM - Prep for tomorrow
14. "See you tomorrow!" 💤

**CTA:** Swipe up to order tomorrow's lunch!

---

### Story Series 2: "This or That?" (Interactive)
**Format:** Poll stickers

**Examples:**
- Thịt kho vs Cá kho? (Vote for tomorrow's menu)
- Cơm trắng vs Cơm gạo lứt?
- Ăn ngay vs Ăn sau 30 phút?
- Ship về nhà vs Ship về office?
- Nhiều rau vs Nhiều thịt?

**Goal:** Engagement + understand customer preferences

---

### Story Series 3: "Myth Busting"
**Format:** Text + Photos

**Topics:**
- "Cơm ship không ngon?" → Show fresh cooking
- "Giao chậm?" → Show delivery timer
- "Không sạch sẽ?" → Show kitchen license
- "Đắt hơn tự nấu?" → Price breakdown
- "Ít món lựa chọn?" → Show 20+ menu items

---

### Story Series 4: "Customer Shoutouts"
**Format:** Repost customer photos/videos

**Content:**
- Unboxing videos
- Food photos
- Reviews screenshots
- Office lunch photos
- "Thank you for ordering!" message

**CTA:** Tag us for a chance to be featured!
