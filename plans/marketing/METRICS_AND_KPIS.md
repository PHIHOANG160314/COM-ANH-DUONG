## 3. CUSTOMER RETENTION METRICS

### 3.1 Repeat Purchase Rate

**Formula:** (Customers who ordered 2+ times / Total customers) x 100

| Month | Repeat Rate Target | Cohort | Orders |
|-------|-------------------|--------|--------|
| 1 | 10% | 300 | 330 |
| 3 | 30% | 1,000 | 1,300 |
| 6 | 50% | 2,000 | 3,000 |

**Tactics to improve:**
- Loyalty program rewards
- Email remarketing
- Personalized offers

---

### 3.2 Order Frequency

**Average orders per customer per month:**

| Customer Tier | Month 1 | Month 3 | Month 6 |
|---------------|---------|---------|---------|
| One-time | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Occasional (2-5/mo) | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Regular (6-10/mo) | 6 | 7 | 8 |
| VIP (11+/mo) | 12 | 15 | 20 |

**Goal:** Move customers up tiers via loyalty incentives

---

### 3.3 Churn Rate

**Formula:** (Customers inactive 30 days / Total customers) x 100

**Target:** <20% monthly churn

**Cohort Analysis Example (Month 3):**
- Month 1 cohort (300 customers): 180 still active = 60% retention
- Month 2 cohort (500 customers): 400 still active = 80% retention

**Win-back Campaign:** Email/SMS to inactive >21 days với special offer

---

### 3.4 Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

**Formula:** Avg Order Value x Order Frequency x Customer Lifespan

**Month 1:**
- AOV: 40k
- Frequency: 1 order/month
- Lifespan: 3 months (projected)
- **LTV: 120,000đ**

**Month 6 (Target):**
- AOV: 50k
- Frequency: 8 orders/month (regulars)
- Lifespan: 12 months
- **LTV: 4,800,000đ**

**LTV:CAC Ratio:**
- Month 1: 120k / 67k = 1.8:1 (breakeven)
- Month 6: 4.8M / 25k = 192:1 (excellent)

## 7. FINANCIAL METRICS

### 7.1 Profitability Metrics

**Gross Margin:**
```
Revenue: 40,000đ/order
COGS (food cost): 18,000đ (45%)
Gross Profit: 22,000đ (55%)
```

**Target:** Maintain 50-55% gross margin

---

**Operating Expenses (per order):**
```
Delivery cost: 5,000đ
Packaging: 2,000đ
Labor (per order): 3,000đ
Marketing (amortized): 2,000đ
Overhead: 2,000đ
Total OpEx: 14,000đ
```

**Net Profit per order:** 8,000đ (20% net margin)

**Target:** 20-25% net margin

---

### 7.2 Cash Flow Metrics

**Daily Cash Flow (Month 3 example):**
```
Revenue: 100 orders x 45,000đ = 4,500,000đ
Daily costs: 100 orders x 32,000đ = 3,200,000đ
Daily profit: 1,300,000đ
Monthly profit: ~39,000,000đ
```

**Break-even point:** 50 orders/day (Month 2)

---

### 7.3 Return on Investment (ROI)

**Marketing ROI:**
```
Month 1:
Marketing spend: 20M
Revenue from marketing: 30M
ROI: (30M - 20M) / 20M = 50%

Month 6:
Marketing spend: 20M
Revenue from marketing: 140M
ROI: (140M - 20M) / 20M = 600%
```

**Target:** >300% ROI by Month 6
