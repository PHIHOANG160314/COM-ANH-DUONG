# Trust Badges & Certifications Research: Cơm Ánh Dương
Date: 2026-02-01
Author: Researcher Agent

## Executive Summary
For a Vietnamese F&B e-commerce site, trust is built through a mix of **mandatory legal compliance** (Bộ Công Thương), **familiar payment logos** (MoMo, ZaloPay), and **visual quality assurances** (Freshness, Hot Delivery). Since COD is the primary payment method, reducing anxiety about product quality upon arrival is paramount.

## 1. Mandatory & Legal Badges (High Priority)

### Bộ Công Thương (Ministry of Industry and Trade)
*   **Type:** Legal Requirement.
*   **Visual:** "Đã thông báo Bộ Công Thương" (Blue Tick).
    *   *Note:* The Blue logo is for "Notification" (websites selling their own goods). The Red logo is for "Registration" (marketplaces). Cơm Ánh Dương needs the **Blue** logo.
*   **Trust Factor:** Extremely High. Vietnamese consumers look for this footer link to verify business legitimacy.
*   **Cost/Action:** Free. Requires submitting business license/household business registration online at `online.gov.vn`.

## 2. Food Safety & Quality Signals

### Official Certifications
*   **VSATTP / ATTP (An Toàn Thực Phẩm):** The standard "Certificate of Food Hygiene and Safety".
    *   *Badge:* Circular emblem with "CỤC AN TOÀN THỰC PHẨM" or "VFA".
    *   *Implementation:* Display the certificate number in the footer or About page.

### "Soft" Trust Badges (Self-Declared)
Effective for visual reassurance without bureaucratic hurdles (must be truthful).
*   **"100% Nguyên Liệu Tươi" (100% Fresh Ingredients):** Green circular badge, leaf iconography.
*   **"Không Chất Bảo Quản" (No Preservatives):** Shield or checkmark icon.
*   **"Bếp Sạch Chuẩn 5 Sao" (5-Star Standard Clean Kitchen):** Chef hat or star icon.
*   **Design Pattern:** Use Green (#2E7D32) for freshness and White/Gold for premium quality.

## 3. Payment & Security (Friction Reduction)

### COD Focus (Primary)
*   **Badge:** "Thanh toán khi nhận hàng" (Cash on Delivery).
*   **Visual:** Icon of a hand receiving a box or money.
*   **Why:** Removes financial risk for the user. Essential for new food brands.

### Digital Payment Logos
Even if COD is default, displaying these implies technical competence and legitimate banking relationships.
*   **Wallets:** **MoMo** (Pink), **ZaloPay** (Green/Blue), **ShopeePay** (Orange).
*   **Banking:** **VNPAY-QR** (Blue/White), **Visa/Mastercard**.
*   **SSL:** "Bảo mật SSL" or a simple Padlock icon in the checkout footer.

## 4. Delivery & Service Guarantees

### Speed & Condition
*   **"Giao Nóng Hổi" (Delivered Hot):** Icon of a steaming bowl/box. Critical for prepared food (Cơm).
*   **"Giao Hàng < 30 Phút":** Timer/Stopwatch icon.
*   **"Bao Đổi Trả":** Money-back guarantee icon if food is spilled/spoiled.

## 5. Implementation Strategy (The "Free" Stack)

### Footer Section Layout (Recommended)
Row 1: **Policies** (Delivery, Returns, Privacy)
Row 2: **Payment Methods** (Row of grayscale or color icons: COD, MoMo, Bank)
Row 3: **Certifications** (Bo Cong Thuong Blue Logo - linked to verify, DMCA Protection)

### Product Page Micro-Badges
Place small icons near the "Add to Cart" button:
1.  [Hot Food Icon] Giao nóng
2.  [Shield Icon] An toàn vệ sinh
3.  [COD Icon] Thanh toán tại nhà
