## COLOR SCHEME (Brand Guidelines)

### Primary Colors:
- **Green (Xanh lá):** #2ECC71 - Main brand color
- **White (Trắng):** #FFFFFF - Background
- **Dark Gray (Xám đậm):** #2C3E50 - Text

### Accent Colors:
- **Orange (Cam):** #E67E22 - Promotions
- **Red (Đỏ):** #E74C3C - Discounts
- **Yellow (Vàng):** #F1C40F - Highlights

---

## FONT RECOMMENDATIONS

### Vietnamese Fonts (Support full diacritics):
1. **UTM Avo** (Logo, Headers)
2. **UTM Nokia** (Body text)
3. **Arial Unicode** (Fallback)

### Print-safe Fonts:
- Avoid: Times New Roman (too formal)
- Use: Open Sans, Montserrat (modern, clean)

---

## DESIGN TIPS

### For Menus:
- Use clear hierarchy (sizes, weights)
- Include photos of top 5 dishes
- White space for readability
- Highlight promotions in color box

### For Flyers:
- Eye-catching headline at top
- Limited text (max 50 words)
- Clear call-to-action (CTA)
- QR code for ordering (optional)

### For Banners:
- Large text visible from 10m away
- Max 3 key messages
- High contrast colors
- Include phone number prominently
